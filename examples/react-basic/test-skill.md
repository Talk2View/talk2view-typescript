# ACME Corp Returns & Refund Policy

## Return Window
- All products can be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- Electronics have a **15-day** return window.
- Sale items are **final sale** and cannot be returned.

## Conditions
- Items must be in original packaging, unopened and unused.
- A valid order number or receipt is required.
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

## Refund Process
1. Customer requests a return via their account or by contacting support.
2. We issue a prepaid return label within 1 business day.
3. Once the item is received and inspected, the refund is processed within **5-7 business days**.
4. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Exchanges
- Exchanges are treated as a return + new order.
- No additional shipping charge for exchanges due to defects.

## Exceptions
- Perishable goods cannot be returned.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.
- Custom/personalized items cannot be returned unless defective.
